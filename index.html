Homi J. Bhabha's three-stage plan

Programme overview

Stage 1

Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors

Use natural uranium to produce power and create plutonium for the next stage.

Stage 2

Fast Breeder Reactors

Use plutonium fuel and breed more fissile material, including uranium-233 from thorium.

Stage 3

Thorium-Based Reactors

Use India's thorium reserves to create long-term, low-carbon energy possibilities.

Video script checklist

What to explain in your project video

  1. Introduce yourself, the topic, and why you selected India's nuclear programme.
  2. Explain the structure of an atom: proton, neutron, electron, nucleus, isotope, and fission.
  3. Show your model and identify Stage 1, Stage 2, and Stage 3.
  4. Explain how uranium, plutonium, and thorium are connected in the programme.
  5. Describe benefits: energy security, low-carbon electricity, scientific progress, and thorium use.
  6. Mention safety, waste handling, and peaceful use of nuclear energy.
  7. End with sources and invite viewers to download your research from the website.
